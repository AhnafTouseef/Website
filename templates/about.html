OUR STORY

We Built the Platform We Wish We Had

In 2023, we graduated with strong grades but not enough practical experience. University courses were full of theory, and we struggled to translate lectures into real code.

Belajar.id started because we wanted a different path: cohort-based learning, real projects, and mentors who answer questions in a way fellow students understand.

We built this platform for affordable, career-relevant study that fits modern student life — with clear steps, practical tools, and a supportive community.

Ahnaf & Shumon — Co-founders · Jashore, 2026
